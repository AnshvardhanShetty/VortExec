# Decisions

A running log of design decisions and rationale. Each entry captures a choice that isn't obvious from the code itself — what was decided, what alternatives were considered, and why this option won.

Format: append-only. Newest entries at the top. Each entry has a date and a short title. Don't edit old entries when you change your mind — add a new entry instead and reference the old one.

The point of this file is twofold. First, future-you will forget why things are the way they are; this is the cheapest way to remember. Second, AI coding assistants reading this file can understand intent, not just structure, which means they make better suggestions.

Five minutes per session of writing here is the highest-leverage documentation habit in the project.

---

## Template

```
## YYYY-MM-DD — Short title

**Decision.** What was decided, in one or two sentences.

**Context.** What was going on that prompted the decision.

**Alternatives.** What else was considered and why it lost.

**Consequences.** What this commits us to. What becomes harder. What becomes easier.
```
